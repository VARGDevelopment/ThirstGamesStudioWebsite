Our game

HOPE: Survival

Top-down co-op survival on a quarantined island. The infected hunt by sound. By day you scavenge for supplies, by night you hold the base against the horde you drew in.

The studio

Veterans, not first-timers

Thirst Games is a small independent team that has spent years making games at bigger studios — and decided to build the survival game we always wanted to play.

Engineering, art, design and production are all in-house. Our strength is deep Unreal Engine work: multiplayer, gameplay systems, combat and optimization.
